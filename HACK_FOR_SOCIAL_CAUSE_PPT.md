### Content Pointers to Put on the Slide:

- **Core Problem Breakdown**:
  - **Information Asymmetry**: Regional college students waste 15–20 hours per semester hunting for unverified notes and illegible photocopies.
  - **Socio-Economic Barrier**: Commercial coaching monopolies charge up to ₹50,000/year, directly widening the rural-urban privilege gap.
  - **Backlog Crisis**: Lack of structured exam preparation leads to high backlog failure rates (25%–35%), delaying graduation and youth employment.
- **Detailed Proposed Solution**:
  - **Centralized Open Courseware**: One-stop structured repository of verified university syllabi, handwritten lecture notes, and digitised previous year papers (PYQs).
  - **Curriculum-Grounded AI Tutor**: Contextual AI built with Google Gemini to break down engineering problems, provide step-by-step math derivations, and generate bilingual explanations.
  - **Exam Intelligence Engine**: Prioritizes frequently repeated topics (Priority A/B/C lists) and structured answering frames.
- **Innovation & Uniqueness**:
  - **Anti-Hallucination Grounding**: Unlike generic ChatGPT, our AI is strictly grounded in official university subject codes, modules, and blueprints.
  - **Inclusive Lightweight Architecture**: Full Progressive Web App (PWA) with offline caching that runs smoothly on low-spec budget devices.

> **🎙️ Speaker Pitch (45-60 Seconds):**  
> *"In grassroots engineering colleges across Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities, academic success is hindered not by student capability, but by information asymmetry and coaching paywalls. Students struggle with 25% to 35% backlog rates because past paper solutions and quality study material are gatekept or scattered. ShikshaSahayak solves this systematically: a centralized, zero-paywall academic platform that pairs verified curriculum resources with a Google Gemini AI tutor tailored specifically for state syllabus patterns. Our uniqueness lies in curriculum grounding—preventing AI hallucinations and delivering lightning-fast, offline-capable learning on budget smartphones."*

### Content Pointers to Put on the Slide:

- **1. Technologies to be Used (Languages, Frameworks, Stack)**:
  - **Programming Languages**: TypeScript 5.8+ (Strict Type Safety end-to-end), JavaScript ES2024, SQL (SQLite D1 dialect).
  - **Frontend UI & Performance**: React 19, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4 (Deep Navy `#0F172A` & Vivid Orange `#EA580C`), Lucide Icons, KaTeX LaTeX math rendering.
  - **Client Resilience**: Progressive Web App (PWA) with Workbox offline service worker caching for seamless studying on budget Android phones (<₹8,000).
  - **Serverless Edge Backend**: Cloudflare Workers & Pages Functions (`functions/api/`) across 300+ global edge locations with sub-50ms TTFB.
  - **Database & Storage**: Dual Cloudflare D1 distributed edge SQLite instances (`DB` for catalog/PYQs & `ASSESSMENT_DB` for LMS) paired with Cloudflare R2 object storage with **$0 bandwidth egress fees**.
  - **AI Router & Inference**: Serverless AI provider router orchestrating Google Gemini 2.5 Flash (SSE streaming tutor), Groq LPU (instant quizzes), with NVIDIA NIM and OpenRouter failover.
- **2. Methodology and Process for Implementation (Flow Charts / Prototype)**:
  - **Stage 1 (Ingestion & Tagging)**: Parsing state university syllabi and digitizing 10+ years of PYQs into a relational subject tree (Branch ➔ Semester ➔ Subject Code ➔ Unit Module ➔ Exam Topic).
  - **Stage 2 (Edge Distribution & $0 Egress Storage)**: Heavy PDFs stored in Cloudflare R2 and delivered via edge CDN, cutting hosting costs to <₹1,500/month for 50,000 active students.
  - **Stage 3 (Curriculum-Grounded AI Prompting)**: Injects official university course codes, syllabus topics, and marking rubrics into prompts to guarantee 0% hallucination.
  - **Stage 4 (Active Recall & Classroom LMS)**: Dynamic quizzes, geofenced mobile attendance for educators, and full offline PWA caching.
  - **Live Working Prototype**: `https://shikshasahayak.org` with dedicated evaluation console at `https://shikshasahayak.org/hackathon` (1-click Student, Teacher, and Admin personas without signup).
- **3. GitHub / GitLab Repository Link**:
  - **Primary Public Repo**: `https://github.com/Kishantakodara/shikshasahayak`
  - **Open-Source License**: MIT License (Civic reusability, verifiable open science, 100% participant IP retention).
  - **Automated Verification**: `npm run hackathon:verify`, `npm run build`, `npm run ai:verify`, `npm run pwa:verify`.
- **4. Demo Video & Walkthrough**:
  - **Video URL**: `https://youtu.be/shikshasahayak-vbyld-demo` (Drive mirror: `shikshasahayak-demo-video`)
  - **Live Interactive Lab**: `https://shikshasahayak.org/hackathon`
  - **3-to-5 Min Walkthrough**: Problem context (0:00-0:45) ➔ Student resource vault & offline PWA (0:45-2:00) ➔ Curriculum-grounded Gemini AI doubts solving & PYQ trends (2:00-3:00) ➔ Teacher geofenced attendance & Admin hub (3:00-3:45) ➔ Serverless edge architecture & cost efficiency (3:45-4:30) ➔ ₹50+ Cr social impact & closing pitch (4:30-5:00).
- **5. Extent of Usage of AI Tools**:
  - **In-Platform Features**: Real-time SSE doubt-clearing with Gemini 2.5 Flash; PYQ frequency prediction (Priority A/B/C); 7-mark structured answering templates; plain English, Hinglish, and Gujarati conceptual breakdowns; sub-second quiz synthesis with Groq.
  - **Engineering & Dev Lifecycle**: AI-assisted university PDF syllabus ingestion; automated test case generation across 14 admin modules; automated WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility and SAST code auditing.
  - **Ethical Guardrails**: Socratic inquiry over direct homework copying; educator peer review of pinned notes; zero student PII leakage to public AI models.

> **🎙️ Speaker Pitch (45-60 Seconds):**  
> *"Our technical approach achieves zero student latency and zero infrastructure egress cost. The frontend is built on React 19, TypeScript, and a Progressive Web App service worker that caches study materials for offline learning on ₹7,000 budget phones. The backend runs on Cloudflare Workers across 300+ edge data centers, querying Cloudflare D1 SQLite in under 30 milliseconds. By hosting thousands of notes and past-paper PDFs in Cloudflare R2, we eliminate bandwidth egress fees, allowing us to support 50,000 active students for under ₹1,500 a month. For intelligence, our server-side router leverages Google Gemini 2.5 Flash and Groq, strictly grounded in state university course codes to provide accurate, bilingual step-by-step problem breakdowns and 10-year PYQ trends. Our full MIT-licensed codebase is on GitHub, and our live production platform includes a 1-click evaluator console for immediate testing."*
